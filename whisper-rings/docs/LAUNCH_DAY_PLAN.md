# Whisper Rings — Launch Day Plan

A concrete, do-this-then-that plan to get from "store is ready" to **first sales**.
Tailored to: US market, ~1-week shipping, no-returns, lingerie ad rules.

> Golden rule for this niche: **trust + tasteful**. Every post and ad leads with
> confidence, fast shipping, and discreet packaging — never crude. That sells *and*
> keeps your ad accounts alive.

---

## T‑minus 7 days — Pre-launch (build an audience to launch *to*)

A launch with no audience = crickets. Spend the week before warming people up.

- [ ] **Final store QA** — test order on each payment method, confirm DSers auto-fulfill, check every size chart, proofread.
- [ ] **Set up the launch discount code** in Shopify → Discounts:
  - Code: **`WHISPER15`** — 15% off, or **free shipping**, first order.
  - Set an expiry (creates urgency): 72 hours after launch.
  - Optional: minimum spend to protect margin (e.g. over $25).
- [ ] **Stand up social profiles** — Instagram + TikTok @whisperrings (or nearest handle). Bio: *"Lingerie that whispers · Fast US shipping ~1 week · Shop ↓"* + link.
- [ ] **Post 3–5 teaser pieces** this week: fabric close-ups, "coming soon," a poll ("which set first?"), behind-the-scenes of your sample unboxing.
- [ ] **Build a waitlist** — drive teaser traffic to the `index.html` coming-soon page; collect emails.
- [ ] **Line up 2–3 micro-influencers** (see outreach template below) to post on/around launch day.
- [ ] **Pre-write everything** — emails, captions, ad copy (templates below) so launch day is just *publish*.
- [ ] **Warm up the ad account** — install pixels, run a tiny $5/day awareness/engagement ad for a few days so the account isn't cold on launch day.

## Launch Day — hour by hour

**Morning**
- [ ] Flip the store live (remove password / coming-soon).
- [ ] Send the **launch email** to your waitlist (template below) with `WHISPER15`.
- [ ] Post the **launch announcement** on Instagram (feed + Stories) and TikTok.
- [ ] Update link-in-bio to the live store.
- [ ] Text/DM friends, family, and your supplier-friend's network the link personally (these are your most likely first buyers).

**Midday**
- [ ] Turn on the **paid test campaign** (details below).
- [ ] Influencers post / you repost their content to Stories.
- [ ] Post a second Story: "We're LIVE 🎉 first 72 hrs: 15% off with WHISPER15."

**Evening**
- [ ] Reply to every comment/DM fast (speed = trust on a new store).
- [ ] Post a "first orders are rolling in 💕" Story for social proof (even 1–2 counts).
- [ ] Check analytics: traffic source, add-to-carts, any checkout drop-off.

## First 72 hours — convert momentum into sales

- [ ] **Daily Stories** counting down the `WHISPER15` expiry (urgency drives the fence-sitters).
- [ ] **Abandoned-cart email** active — recover the "almost" buyers.
- [ ] **Watch the funnel:** lots of visits, no add-to-cart → pricing/photos/offer issue. Add-to-cart, no checkout → shipping cost or trust issue.
- [ ] **Fulfill orders same day** in DSers; send a warm thank-you + tracking.
- [ ] **Ask first buyers for a photo review** (offer a small next-order discount).
- [ ] **Double down** on whatever channel produced the first sale; pause what's dead.

## Paid test campaign (lingerie-compliant)

> Read `AD_GUIDANCE.md` first. Keep creative tasteful — no skin-focus crops, models
> fully in the product, target 18+.

- [ ] **Platform:** start with **Meta (Instagram-placement)** — best for lingerie visuals. Use TikTok *organically* (paid is restrictive).
- [ ] **Budget:** small to find a winner — e.g. **$10–20/day**, 3–5 days, before scaling.
- [ ] **Structure:** 1 campaign → 3 ad sets (broad US women 18+, lookalike later) → test your 3 hero products.
- [ ] **Creative:** flat-lay or on-model lifestyle, headline *"Fast US shipping · ~1 week"* + *"15% off with WHISPER15"*.
- [ ] **Objective:** Sales/Conversions if the pixel has data; otherwise start with Traffic/Engagement to warm it.
- [ ] **Retarget:** add-to-cart and page-viewers who didn't buy — usually your cheapest sales.
- [ ] **Read at 72h:** keep the winning product+audience, cut the rest.

## Metrics to watch (first week)

| Metric | Healthy signal | If it's off |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| Add-to-cart rate | ~5–10% of visits | Low → photos/price/offer |
| Checkout completion | ~30–50% of carts | Low → shipping cost / trust |
| Cost per purchase | < your profit/order | High → creative or targeting |
| First reviews | collect ASAP | None → email buyers, incentivize |

## First-sale checklist (don't fumble it)

- [ ] Fulfill in DSers same day.
- [ ] Send a personal thank-you + tracking.
- [ ] Screenshot it (anonymized) for a social proof Story.
- [ ] Note the source — that's where sale #2 comes from.
- [ ] Request a photo review after delivery.
