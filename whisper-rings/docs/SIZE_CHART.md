# Whisper Rings — Size Guide

⚠️ **The single most important page on the store.** With no returns/exchanges,
every sizing mistake is a refund you can't recover. Asian-cut lingerie (Ellolace/
AliExpress) typically **runs 1–2 sizes smaller than US sizing**, so the rule is:
**when in doubt, size up.** Always verify these numbers against your supplier's
actual measurements before publishing — confirm each SKU.

---

## How to measure (put this on the page)

Use a soft tape measure, against your body, not over clothing.

- **Bust:** around the fullest part of your chest.
- **Underbust / band:** snugly under the bust.
- **Waist:** narrowest part of the torso.
- **Hips:** around the fullest part.

> Tip: between two sizes? **Choose the larger** — Whisper Rings styles run small.
