# Whisper Rings — First 30 Days Growth Plan

What to do *after* launch day to turn first sales into a repeatable, profitable
machine. Tailored to: US market, ~1-week shipping, no-returns, lingerie ad rules.

> The goal of month 1 isn't to get rich — it's to **find one product + one channel
> that reliably produces profitable sales**, then pour fuel on it. Track everything;
> let data, not feelings, decide what to scale.

---

## The 4 weekly themes

| Week | Focus | Win condition |
|------|-------|---------------|
| 1 | **Stabilize & listen** | First sales in, fulfillment smooth, data flowing |
| 2 | **Find the winner** | Identify 1 best product + 1 best channel |
| 3 | **Scale what works** | Increase spend/output on the winner, raise AOV |
| 4 | **Systematize & retain** | Email flows live, reviews coming in, repeat buyers |

---

## Week 1 — Stabilize & Listen

- [ ] **Fulfill every order same day** in DSers; confirm tracking emails send.
- [ ] **Reply to every DM/comment/email within hours** — early trust compounds.
- [ ] **Keep the launch energy going** — daily Story, countdown to `WHISPER15` expiry.
- [ ] **Watch the funnel daily** (GA4 + Shopify analytics):
  - Visits → add-to-cart → checkout → purchase. Find the biggest drop-off.
- [ ] **Log first-buyer data:** which product, which traffic source, which ad/post.
- [ ] **Fix friction fast** — confusing size info, slow page, scary shipping line, missing trust badge.
- [ ] **Request photo reviews** from the first buyers as they receive orders (offer 10% off next).
- [ ] **Kill obvious losers** — pause ads/products with traffic but zero interest.

**End-of-week review:** Which product got the most add-to-carts? Which channel
(organic IG, TikTok, ads, email, DMs) drove the most sales? Write it down.

## Week 2 — Find the Winner

- [ ] **Concentrate on your top 1–3 products** — feature them everywhere; deprioritize the rest.
- [ ] **Order more samples** of the winners and **shoot your own photos/video** — UGC-style content outperforms stock and ads better.
- [ ] **Double down on the winning channel:**
  - *Organic winning?* Post 1–2× daily; lean into the format that hit (try-on styling, fabric ASMR, "get ready with me").
  - *Ads winning?* Cut losing ad sets, keep the winning product+audience, add fresh creative.
- [ ] **Turn on retargeting** — site visitors + add-to-cart non-buyers (cheapest sales you'll get).
- [ ] **Start collecting emails on-site** — popup with a small first-order incentive.
- [ ] **Reach out to 5–10 more micro-influencers** with free product (gifting > paid early on).
- [ ] **Add reviews to product pages** as they come in (Loox/Judge.me with photos).

**End-of-week review:** Do you have a product with a repeatable cost-per-sale below
your profit margin? If yes → scale (Week 3). If not → test new product/creative/angle.

## Week 3 — Scale What Works

- [ ] **Scale the winning ad** — raise budget gradually (~20–30% every couple days; big jumps reset learning). Or build a lookalike audience from purchasers.
- [ ] **Refresh creative weekly** — lingerie ads fatigue fast; rotate 2–3 new tasteful variations to avoid the account flagging repeats.
- [ ] **Raise average order value (AOV):**
  - **Bundles** — "Any 2 sets for $X."
  - **Free-shipping threshold** — "Free US shipping over $50."
  - **Post-purchase upsell** app (one-click add after checkout).
- [ ] **Expand the catalog** — add 5–10 more products *similar to the winner* (same vibe/category that's selling).
- [ ] **Keep organic cadence** — consistency beats virality; show up daily.
- [ ] **Watch margins, not just revenue** — track profit per order after product + shipping + ad cost. Scaling unprofitable sales burns cash.

**End-of-week review:** Is profit growing as you scale, or is cost-per-sale creeping
up? Hold spend where it's still profitable.

## Week 4 — Systematize & Retain

> Repeat customers are pure margin — no ad cost. Set up the machine that brings them back.

- [ ] **Email flows live** (Shopify Email / Klaviyo):
  - **Welcome** (popup signup → discount)
  - **Abandoned cart** (1–3 emails)
  - **Post-purchase** (thank-you, care tips, review request)
  - **Win-back** (30 days later, "we miss you" offer)
- [ ] **Send a monthly campaign** — new arrivals or a themed promo to your whole list.
- [ ] **Build a reviews wall / UGC highlight** — social proof that sells passively.
- [ ] **Document your SOPs** — order fulfillment, customer-service replies, posting schedule. Future-you (or a VA) will thank you.
- [ ] **Negotiate with the supplier** (via your contact) — better pricing / priority shipping now that you have volume; line up backups for bestsellers.
- [ ] **Plan month 2** off the data: best product, best channel, best offer → do more of it.

## What to track (simple weekly scoreboard)

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Orders | ↑ week over week | volume trend |
| Conversion rate | 1–3%+ | <1% = funnel/trust issue |
| Avg order value (AOV) | ↑ via bundles | raise with offers |
| Cost per purchase (ads) | < profit/order | the make-or-break number |
| Profit per order | positive & growing | after product+ship+ads |
| Email list size | growing | future free sales |
| Reviews collected | steady | trust engine |
| Repeat-customer rate | building by wk 4 | retention signal |

## Common month-1 problems & fixes

- **Traffic but no sales** → photos, price, trust badges, or the offer. A/B the hero image and headline.
- **Add-to-carts but no checkout** → shipping cost shock or trust. Show "fast US shipping" + secure badges earlier.
- **Sales but no profit** → markup too low or ad cost too high. Raise prices/AOV, tighten targeting.
- **Ad account warning** → creative too revealing. Re-read `AD_GUIDANCE.md`, swap to lifestyle/flat-lay.
- **Size complaints** → tighten the size chart immediately; with no returns this is your biggest risk.
- **"Where's my order?"** → proactive tracking emails + a visible shipping-time banner.

## North-star for the month

> **One profitable product + one reliable channel + an email list that brings people
> back.** Nail that in 30 days and month 2 is about scaling, not guessing.
