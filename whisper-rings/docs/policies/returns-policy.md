## Intimate apparel — no returns or exchanges

For health and hygiene reasons, **all sales of intimate apparel are final.** We
cannot accept returns or exchanges on lingerie, bodysuits, underwear, or any
intimate item that has been delivered. Please use our [Size Guide](#) and check
your measurements carefully before ordering — our styles run small, so when in
doubt, size up.

By completing your purchase you acknowledge and agree to this hygiene policy.

## If something is wrong, we'll make it right

We stand behind every order. If your item arrives **defective, damaged, or
incorrect**, contact us within **48 hours of delivery** at
[hello@whisper-rings.com] with:

- Your order number
- A photo of the item and the issue

We will arrange a **free replacement or a full refund** for verified defective,
damaged, or wrong items. (This does not apply to fit/size preference, which is
covered by the no-exchange policy above.)

## Refunds

Approved refunds (for defective/incorrect items) are issued to your original
payment method within **5–10 business days** of approval.

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund within **2 hours** of purchase, before
they enter fulfillment. After that, we may have already processed your order.

## Questions

Email us at [hello@whisper-rings.com] — we're happy to help you choose the right
size *before* you buy.
