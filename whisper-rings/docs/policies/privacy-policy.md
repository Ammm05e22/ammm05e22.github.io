Whisper Rings ("we," "us," "our") operates whisper-rings.com (the "Site"). This
policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Order information:** name, shipping/billing address, email, phone, and payment
  details (processed securely by our payment provider — we do **not** store full
  card numbers).
- **Account information:** if you create an account, your login details and order history.
- **Usage data:** pages visited, device/browser info, and cookies, collected via
  analytics and advertising tools (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel).

## How we use it

- To process and ship your orders and provide support.
- To send order updates and, with your consent, marketing emails (unsubscribe anytime).
- To improve the Site and measure our advertising.
- To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

## Sharing

We share data only with providers that help us run the store:

- **Payment processor** ([Shopify Payments / Stripe / PayPal])
- **Fulfillment / shipping partners** (to deliver your order)
- **Analytics & advertising** ([Google, Meta, TikTok])

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Cookies

We use cookies for cart functionality, analytics, and advertising. You can control
cookies in your browser settings; a consent banner is provided where required.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. California), you may request access to, correction
of, or deletion of your personal data. Email [hello@whisper-rings.com] to make a request.

## Data retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed for orders, legal, and accounting purposes, and
use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

## Children

The Site is intended for adults **18+**. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
