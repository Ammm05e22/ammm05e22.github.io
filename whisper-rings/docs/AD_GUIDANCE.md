# Whisper Rings — Advertising Without Getting Banned

Intimate apparel is allowed on the major platforms, but they enforce strict rules.
One non-compliant ad can restrict your whole account **before your first sale**.
Stay tasteful from day one — it matches the brand *and* keeps you running.

> This is practical guidance, not official policy. Always read each platform's
> current rules before spending.

---

## Universal rules (apply everywhere)

- **No bare skin focus / nudity / sheer see-through close-ups.**
- **No tight crops of breasts, buttocks, or crotch.** Show the full styled look.
- **No "before/after," weight-loss, or body-shaming angles.**
- **Target 18+** only.
- Lead with **confidence, comfort, fabric, fit, fast shipping** — not "sexy."
- Models fully wearing the product, relaxed poses, lifestyle context.

## Meta (Facebook / Instagram)

- Lingerie ads are allowed but heavily reviewed. Keep imagery editorial/lifestyle.
- Avoid suggestive text and excessive skin. Product-on-model or flat-lay works best.
- Expect some rejections; appeal politely, don't re-upload the same rejected creative repeatedly.
- Warm up a new ad account: start with low spend, build slowly.

## TikTok

- **Most restrictive** for intimates. Paid lingerie ads are often disallowed or
  limited. **Organic content usually outperforms paid here.**
- Use styling tips, fabric close-ups, packaging/unboxing, confidence storytelling —
  not on-body reveals.
- Read TikTok's ad policy before spending a cent.

## Google

- **Search ads:** fine for non-explicit lingerie with tasteful landing pages.
- **Shopping:** non-explicit lingerie is generally allowed; some items may need the
  "adult" flag. Keep product images clean (no suggestive posing).

## Compliant creative ideas

- Flat-lay sets on cream/blush backgrounds with the wordmark.
- Detail shots: lace, satin, straps, stitching.
- "1-week US shipping" and "discreet packaging" as headline hooks.
- UGC-style: a confident, fully-clothed-over-lingerie lifestyle shot.
- Testimonials / reviews once you have them.

## Test budget approach

1. Pick 3–5 hero products.
2. Run small, clean ad sets to find 1 winner (product + audience).
3. Scale only the winner; keep creative compliant as you grow.

## If an account gets restricted

- Don't panic or spin up fake accounts (that compounds bans).
- Review the policy cited, fix the creative, appeal once, calmly.
- Lean on organic social + email while you sort it out.
