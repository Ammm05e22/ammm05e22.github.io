## Pricing model (US dropshipping)

Use **landed cost × 3** as your baseline retail, rounded to a charm price (.99 / .95),
then set a higher **compare-at** to show a discount.

```
Retail ≈ (product cost + shipping) × 3   →   round to $X9.99
Compare-at ≈ Retail × 1.5
```

Example: $9 landed × 3 = $27 → list at **$29.99**, compare-at **$44.99**.
The suggested prices below assume ~$8–14 landed cost; adjust to your real numbers.

## Launch tips for these 10

- **Pick 3 "hero" products** to feature on the homepage and test in ads (e.g. #1, #2, #5
  tend to convert well as visuals).
- **Photograph your samples** for at least the hero products — real photos crush
  AliExpress stock images for trust and ad performance.
- **Group into collections:** *Sets*, *Bodysuits & Teddies*, *Robes & Lounge*.
- **Bundle idea:** "Any 2 sets for $XX" raises average order value and helps cover ad cost.
- **Double-check each size chart** against the supplier's real measurements before publishing.
