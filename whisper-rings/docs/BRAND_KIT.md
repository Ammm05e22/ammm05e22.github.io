# Whisper Rings — Brand Kit

The foundation every page, listing, and ad should follow. Goal: **soft, sensual,
premium, confident — never crude.** This tone also keeps your ad accounts alive.

---

## Brand essence

- **Name:** Whisper Rings
- **Domain:** whisper-rings.com
- **One-line promise:** *Beautiful lingerie, fast US shipping, delivered in about a week.*
- **Positioning:** Elevated, body-confident intimates for everyday self-expression —
  accessible luxury, not bargain-bin and not explicit.
- **Who it's for:** Women (and gifting partners) who want to feel confident and
  comfortable; values quality, discretion, and fast delivery.

## Voice & tone

- Warm, elegant, encouraging. Speak to confidence and comfort, not just "sexy."
- Short, sensory sentences. Avoid crude language and clichés.
- ✅ "Soft against your skin, made to be seen — or kept just for you."
- ❌ "Hot sexy lingerie cheap sale!!!"

## Typography

- **Headings:** an elegant serif — *Cormorant Garamond* or *Playfair Display*.
- **Body:** a clean sans — *Inter*, *Nunito Sans*, or *Montserrat*.
- Generous line spacing, lots of whitespace. Lowercase or small-caps for a soft feel.

## Logo direction

- Wordmark "Whisper Rings" in the serif, optionally with a thin circular "ring" mark.
- Keep it monochrome (espresso or wine on cream) for versatility; gold for premium accents.

## Taglines (pick one as primary)

1. *Softly seen.*
2. *For the confidence beneath.*
3. *Lingerie that whispers.*
4. *Beautifully yours — fast.*

## Trust pillars (repeat across the site)

- 🚚 **Fast US shipping** — delivered in ~1 week.
- 🔒 **Discreet packaging & checkout.**
- 📏 **True-fit size guide** so you order right the first time.
- 💬 **Real human support.**

## Do / Don't

- ✅ Tasteful lifestyle imagery, fabric/detail close-ups, confidence-led copy.
- ✅ Consistent palette and fonts on every page.
- ❌ Explicit/nude crops, body-shaming "before/after," AliExpress stock copy, watermarked photos.
