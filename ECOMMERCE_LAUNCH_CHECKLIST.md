# E-Commerce Launch Checklist — From Zero to First Sale

A practical, ordered checklist for taking a standard online store from an idea to its
first paying customer. Work top to bottom; later phases assume earlier ones are done.

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation & Business Setup

- [ ] **Pick a niche / product line** and confirm there is real demand (search volume, competitors, communities).
- [ ] **Validate the product** — talk to 5–10 potential buyers, run a small pre-order or landing-page test.
- [ ] **Source or produce the product** — supplier, manufacturer, dropshipper, or self-made.
- [ ] **Calculate unit economics** — cost of goods, shipping, fees, and target margin (aim for healthy gross margin).
- [ ] **Register the business** — sole proprietorship / LLC / company, as appropriate for your region.
- [ ] **Get a tax ID / VAT registration** if required.
- [ ] **Open a business bank account** to keep finances separate.
- [ ] **Choose a brand name** and check that the domain + social handles are available.

## Phase 2 — Brand & Domain

- [ ] **Buy the domain** (prefer `.com` or a strong local TLD).
- [ ] **Design a logo** and pick brand colors + fonts.
- [ ] **Set up a business email** (e.g. `hello@yourbrand.com`).
- [ ] **Write your brand story / "About" copy** — why you exist, who you serve.

## Phase 3 — Store Platform & Build

- [ ] **Choose a platform** — Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Squarespace, or custom.
- [ ] **Pick and customize a theme** that is clean, fast, and mobile-first.
- [ ] **Add core pages:**
  - [ ] Home
  - [ ] Product / collection pages
  - [ ] Product detail page (with variants, stock, CTA)
  - [ ] Cart
  - [ ] Checkout
  - [ ] About
  - [ ] Contact
  - [ ] FAQ
- [ ] **Add legal / policy pages:**
  - [ ] Shipping policy
  - [ ] Returns & refunds policy
  - [ ] Privacy policy
  - [ ] Terms of service
  - [ ] Cookie / consent notice (GDPR/CCPA as applicable)
- [ ] **Configure navigation** — header menu, footer links, search.
- [ ] **Ensure mobile responsiveness** and fast load times.

## Phase 4 — Product Listings

- [ ] **Take high-quality product photos** (multiple angles, lifestyle shots, zoom).
- [ ] **Write compelling product titles and descriptions** (benefits + specs + keywords).
- [ ] **Set prices and any compare-at / sale prices.**
- [ ] **Configure variants** (size, color, etc.) and SKUs.
- [ ] **Set inventory levels** and low-stock alerts.
- [ ] **Add product weights/dimensions** for accurate shipping rates.

## Phase 5 — Payments

- [ ] **Connect a payment gateway** — Stripe, PayPal, or platform-native payments.
- [ ] **Enable common methods** — cards, digital wallets (Apple/Google Pay), local options.
- [ ] **Verify your payout/bank details.**
- [ ] **Place a real test order** end to end and confirm the money flows correctly.
- [ ] **Set up tax calculation** (automatic where possible).

## Phase 6 — Shipping & Fulfillment

- [ ] **Define shipping zones and rates** (flat, real-time carrier, or free-over-threshold).
- [ ] **Choose carriers** and set up accounts.
- [ ] **Decide on packaging** and order any branded materials.
- [ ] **Set up order notifications and shipping confirmations.**
- [ ] **Document your fulfillment process** (pick, pack, label, ship) or connect a 3PL.
- [ ] **Set processing-time expectations** on the site.

## Phase 7 — Trust & Conversion

- [ ] **Install an SSL certificate** (https everywhere — usually automatic on hosted platforms).
- [ ] **Display trust signals** — secure-checkout badges, payment logos, guarantees.
- [ ] **Add clear contact options** — email, contact form, ideally live chat.
- [ ] **Show social proof** — reviews, testimonials, ratings (seed with early customers).
- [ ] **Optimize the checkout** — guest checkout, minimal fields, progress indicator.
- [ ] **Set up abandoned-cart recovery** emails.

## Phase 8 — Analytics & Tracking

- [ ] **Install web analytics** (Google Analytics 4 or similar).
- [ ] **Set up conversion / purchase tracking.**
- [ ] **Add the Meta Pixel / TikTok Pixel / Google Ads tag** if you'll run paid ads.
- [ ] **Configure Google Search Console** and submit a sitemap.
- [ ] **Verify events fire** on add-to-cart, checkout, and purchase.

## Phase 9 — Pre-Launch QA

- [ ] **Proofread all copy** for typos and broken links.
- [ ] **Test on multiple devices and browsers.**
- [ ] **Place full test orders** for each payment method.
- [ ] **Test discount codes** and gift cards if used.
- [ ] **Confirm all automated emails send** (order, shipping, password reset).
- [ ] **Check page speed** and fix slow/heavy assets.
- [ ] **Remove placeholder content** and any "test" products.
- [ ] **Set up a 404 page** and basic redirects.

## Phase 10 — Launch & First Sale

- [ ] **Announce to your network** — friends, family, email list, social media.
- [ ] **Post on your social channels** with product highlights.
- [ ] **Offer a launch promo** — discount code, free shipping, or bundle.
- [ ] **Reach out to your existing audience / waitlist.**
- [ ] **List on relevant marketplaces or communities** where your buyers hang out.
- [ ] **Run a small, targeted paid ad** to test demand (optional but effective).
- [ ] **Ask early supporters to share** and leave honest reviews.
- [ ] 🎉 **Make your first sale** — then fulfill it fast and ask for feedback.

## Phase 11 — Post-First-Sale (Keep the Momentum)

- [ ] **Thank the customer** and request a review/photo.
- [ ] **Review analytics** — where did the buyer come from? Double down on it.
- [ ] **Fix any friction** the first order revealed.
- [ ] **Set up email marketing flows** — welcome, post-purchase, win-back.
- [ ] **Plan restocks and inventory** based on early demand.
- [ ] **Iterate on pricing, copy, and ads** using real data.

---

### Quick "Minimum to Sell" Shortlist
If you only have a weekend, do at least these:

1. Domain + store platform set up
2. One great product listing with good photos
3. Working payment gateway (tested)
4. Shipping rate configured
5. Refund + privacy policy pages
6. Analytics installed
7. Announce to your network with a launch offer
