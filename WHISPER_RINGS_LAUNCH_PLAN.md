# whisper-rings.com — Lingerie Dropshipping Launch Plan

A tailored, zero-to-first-sale plan for **whisper-rings.com**, dropshipping lingerie
from an AliExpress supplier (Ellolace). This builds on the generic
`ECOMMERCE_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` but focuses on what's specific to **dropshipping**
and **intimate apparel** — the two things that make or break this niche.

**Confirmed parameters for this store:**
- **Market:** United States only.
- **Shipping:** ~1 week (you have fast shippers) — this is a real competitive
  advantage; feature it prominently ("Ships from the US / 1-week delivery").
- **Returns:** No returns (hygiene policy for intimate apparel) — see Phase 6 for
  how to state this so it converts and survives card disputes.

> ⚠️ With fast shipping and no returns settled, your remaining two big risks are:
> **bad sizing** (your top complaint + chargeback source) and **ad accounts banned
> for "adult" content**. Most of this plan now exists to defuse those two.

---

## Phase 0 — Validate the Supplier (do this FIRST)

- [ ] **Order 3–5 sample pieces from Ellolace yourself** before listing anything. Photograph them, check real quality, fit, and fabric.
- [ ] **Confirm AliExpress sizing is Asian sizing** — it almost always runs 1–2 sizes small vs US. This is your #1 future complaint source, especially with a no-returns policy.
- [ ] **Lock in the fast (≤1 week to US) shipping line with your supplier contact** — confirm it in writing, confirm it covers your hero SKUs, and confirm a backup if a line slows down. Your whole brand promise rests on this.
- [ ] **Check the supplier's stats** — store rating (aim 95%+), years active, review count, "ships from" location (a US warehouse is ideal for your 1-week promise).
- [ ] **Check stock depth** — dropship items vanish; pick suppliers with consistent inventory and have backup SKUs ready.
- [ ] **Confirm "Ellolace" isn't a protected trademark** you'd be infringing — sell the products, not someone else's brand name. Your brand is Whisper Rings.
- [ ] **Ask the supplier (via your friend's contact)** about: blind/white-label shipping (no AliExpress invoices in the parcel), bulk pricing, and faster shipping lines.

## Phase 1 — Business & Money

- [ ] **Register the business** and open a separate bank account / payment account.
- [ ] **Calculate unit economics** with dropship reality:
  - Product cost (AliExpress) + shipping + transaction fees + ad cost per sale.
  - **Target retail = 2.5–3.5× your landed cost.** Lingerie supports strong markups; you'll need margin to cover ad spend and refunds.
- [ ] **Budget for ads + samples + refunds** — keep a buffer; first sales usually come from paid traffic.
- [ ] **Decide return policy** (see Phase 6 — intimates are special).

## Phase 2 — Brand: "Whisper Rings"

- [ ] **Confirm domain** whisper-rings.com is connected and on HTTPS.
- [ ] **Define the brand vibe** — the name reads soft, sensual, intimate, premium. Pick a palette (e.g. blush/nude/black/cream), an elegant font, and a tasteful tone. Lean classy, not crude — it keeps ad accounts alive and builds trust.
- [ ] **Logo + favicon.**
- [ ] **Business email** (e.g. `hello@whisper-rings.com`).
- [ ] **Write an "About" story** — why Whisper Rings, who it's for (confidence, self-expression, comfort).

## Phase 3 — Store Build

- [ ] **Pick a platform** — Shopify is the default for AliExpress dropshipping (best app ecosystem). WooCommerce works if you want lower fees + more control.
- [ ] **Install a clean, fast, mobile-first theme** (most lingerie buyers are on mobile).
- [ ] **Install a dropshipping app** to import products & auto-forward orders to the supplier:
  - [ ] **DSers** (official AliExpress partner, free tier) — recommended, or
  - [ ] AutoDS / Zendrop / CJdropshipping as alternatives.
- [ ] **Core pages:** Home, Collections (Bras, Sets, Bodysuits, etc.), Product detail, Cart, Checkout, About, Contact, FAQ.
- [ ] **Legal/policy pages** (see Phase 6).
- [ ] **Add an age-appropriate presentation** — keep imagery tasteful; consider a light "must be 18+" note if your jurisdiction/ads require it.

## Phase 4 — Product Listings (the make-or-break step)

- [ ] **Import a focused starter catalog** — 15–30 hero products, not hundreds. Curate the best Ellolace pieces from your samples.
- [ ] **Rewrite every title & description** — never keep AliExpress's broken-English copy. Write benefit-led, brand-voice copy.
- [ ] **Replace/clean up images** — remove watermarks and any competitor branding; use consistent, tasteful photos. Your own sample photos build huge trust here.
- [ ] **Build a clear, accurate SIZE CHART** with **cm/inch measurements**, not just S/M/L. Add a "this brand runs small — size up" note. This single step prevents most returns.
- [ ] **Set prices** at your 2.5–3.5× markup.
- [ ] **Add the fast-shipping line** to each product page ("Fast US shipping — ~1 week").
- [ ] **Map each product to the supplier SKU** in DSers so orders auto-fulfill.

## Phase 5 — Payments

- [ ] **Connect a gateway.** ⚠️ Note: some processors classify lingerie/intimates as "high-risk adult." **Shopify Payments and Stripe generally allow tasteful lingerie**, but read their prohibited-business lists; avoid explicit/adult-toy positioning. Keep PayPal as a backup but know they can hold funds on new high-risk stores.
- [ ] **Enable cards + Apple/Google Pay + PayPal.**
- [ ] **Place a full real test order** end to end (including the auto-fulfillment to AliExpress) and confirm money + order flow.
- [ ] **Configure taxes** for your region.

## Phase 6 — Shipping, Returns & Trust (intimates are special)

- [ ] **Feature the 1-week US shipping as a selling point** — homepage banner, product pages, cart: "Fast US shipping — delivered in ~1 week." This beats nearly every AliExpress-based competitor and lifts conversion.
- [ ] **Still set a safe, honest range** (e.g. "5–9 business days") rather than promising an exact day, so a slow parcel doesn't trigger a dispute.
- [ ] **Offer order tracking** (app like 17track/AfterShip) so customers can self-serve "where's my order?".
- [ ] **Write the no-returns policy carefully.** A blanket "no returns" is legal in the US, but two cautions:
  - **Always make an exception for defective / wrong / damaged items** — refund or replace these. Refusing creates chargebacks and bad reviews, and may run afoul of consumer-protection expectations.
  - **Card networks & PayPal can force a chargeback regardless of your policy.** A clearly posted hygiene policy is your defense evidence, not an absolute shield.
  - Recommended wording: *"For hygiene reasons we cannot accept returns or exchanges on intimate apparel. If your item arrives defective or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours and we'll make it right."* Show it at checkout (a tick-to-agree helps in disputes).
- [ ] **Because there are no size exchanges, the size chart must be airtight** (see Phase 4) — sizing mistakes become refunds you can't recover.
- [ ] **Trust signals are critical for lingerie + dropshipping:** secure-checkout badges, payment logos, real contact info, a visible FAQ covering sizing/shipping/returns.
- [ ] **Add reviews** — seed with your sample experience; collect customer reviews early (app like Loox/Judge.me with photos).
- [ ] **Set up abandoned-cart emails.**

## Phase 7 — Analytics & Pixels

- [ ] **Install GA4** + Search Console (submit sitemap).
- [ ] **Install Meta Pixel and/or TikTok Pixel** for ad tracking and retargeting.
- [ ] **Verify purchase/add-to-cart events fire.**

## Phase 8 — Advertising WITHOUT getting banned (lingerie-specific)

> Meta and TikTok have strict rules on intimate apparel. Break them and your ad
> account gets restricted before you make a sale. Play it clean from day one.

- [ ] **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):** lingerie ads are allowed but **must not** focus on bare skin, use close-up crops of body parts, imply nudity, or use "before/after"/body-shaming angles. Show product context, lifestyle, confidence. Target 18+. Expect tighter review.
- [ ] **TikTok:** very restrictive on intimates — organic content (try-on hauls within guidelines, styling tips, brand storytelling) often works better than paid. Read TikTok's ad policy before spending.
- [ ] **Google Shopping:** mark the store correctly; "adult-oriented" lingerie may need the adult content flag — non-explicit lingerie is usually fine in standard Shopping.
- [ ] **Keep all creative tasteful** — it protects every ad account and matches the Whisper Rings brand.
- [ ] **Start with a small test budget** (e.g. a few small ad sets) to find a winning product + audience before scaling.

## Phase 9 — Pre-Launch QA

- [ ] **Test orders** on every payment method + confirm auto-fulfillment to supplier.
- [ ] **Proofread all copy**, check links, remove any "test"/placeholder products.
- [ ] **Test on mobile** (most of your traffic).
- [ ] **Confirm all emails send** (order, shipping/tracking, support).
- [ ] **Re-check every size chart.**
- [ ] **Page speed** — compress images.

## Phase 10 — Launch & First Sale

- [ ] **Set up Instagram + TikTok for Whisper Rings** — this niche is visual and social-first.
- [ ] **Post tasteful product/lifestyle content** and build a small following before/with launch.
- [ ] **Announce to your network** and your friend's contacts.
- [ ] **Launch offer** — a first-order discount or free shipping over a threshold.
- [ ] **Run the small Meta/TikTok test campaign** (clean creative) to drive first traffic.
- [ ] **Partner with a micro-influencer** in the lingerie/body-positivity space for a first wave of social proof (high ROI in this niche).
- [ ] 🎉 **First sale** — fulfill it immediately in DSers, confirm tracking, and over-communicate with the buyer.

## Phase 11 — After the First Sale

- [ ] **Ask the customer for a photo review** (offer a small incentive).
- [ ] **Watch which product + ad + audience produced the sale** and double down.
- [ ] **Order more samples of your winners** for better content.
- [ ] **Build email flows** — welcome, shipping-reassurance, post-purchase, win-back.
- [ ] **Track refund/return reasons** — if it's sizing, fix the chart immediately.
- [ ] **Negotiate with the supplier** (via your contact) for faster shipping / better pricing as volume grows; consider a faster-shipping or domestic-warehouse supplier once a product proves itself.
